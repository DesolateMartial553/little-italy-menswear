Letters & questions

Common Questions

What customers usually ask before visiting.

How long does made-to-measure take?

Typically 2-3 weeks depending on the garment and any alterations needed — so if it's for a wedding or prom, call us as early as you can.

Can I use any store for alterations?

Yes, all three stores offer alteration services regardless of where you bought the item.

Do you offer wedding party fittings?

Yes, we regularly fit full wedding parties — book ahead for group appointments.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead is recommended for fittings and alterations. Call 0121 478 0042 to book.
